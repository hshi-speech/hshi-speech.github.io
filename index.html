Hao Shi 史 昊

Research Scientist, Shanda AI Research Tokyo

I work on speech and language AI at Shanda Group Corp, after a Ph.D. in Informatics at Kyoto University. My research covers robust speech processing, multi-talker ASR, and speech-language modeling.

The problem I keep returning to is how speech models can use strong learned priors while staying faithful to what was actually said: recovering speech from noise, attributing it to the right talker, and preserving pronunciation in Japanese ASR and TTS.
